My background is in journalism and data visualization. I've worked on digital storytelling at NPR and Business Insider, and now I'm exploring machine learning and building better products.

Why I made this

I've listened to Perfect Person since Episode 1. With all the perfect advice Miles and his guests give, it's hard to recall which episode covered a specific topic. I built Perfect Person Archive to make those moments easier to rediscover. It's also an opportunity for me to explore how natural language processing can make information more accessible and useful.
